Noodles

Konjac Instant Noodles

NET WT. 15 OZ (426 G) · 2 servings per pouch

Springy, smooth konjac noodles with a sesame sauce packet and a soy sauce packet.

  • Ready to eat. No cooking needed.
  • 2 sauce packets inside: sesame and soy.
  • Shelf stable. Best within 18 months unopened.
  • Vegan 100%.
How to enjoy

Gently knead the clear noodle bag to loosen the strands, open it, toss with the sauce packets and eat. Add your own sliced veggies if you like.

Contains wheat, soy, sesame.

Plant-Based Sashimi

Our Story

Vegan + Alchemy

VEGALCHEMY is a plant-based food brand from Taiwan. The name joins “vegan” and “alchemy”: the craft of turning simple plant ingredients into food that surprises you.

We make it for people who don’t usually eat vegan. No lecture, just something you’ll want to open and eat.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How should I store them?

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Both products are shelf stable and best within 18 months unopened. Check the printed date on the pouch.

How do I eat the Konjac Instant Noodles?

They’re ready to eat, with no cooking needed. Gently knead the clear noodle bag to loosen the strands, open it, toss with the two sauce packets (sesame and soy) and eat.

How do I serve the Plant-Based Sashimi?

Refrigerate for 4 hours, then slice and serve. It’s best with soy sauce and wasabi, which are not included in the pouch.

Are they vegan? Do they contain allergens?

Both are Vegan 100%, and the sashimi is fish-free. The noodles contain wheat, soy and sesame. The sashimi contains soy. Always check the label on your pouch before eating.

Do they contain onion or garlic?

Neither product’s ingredient list includes onion, garlic, leek or chives. See the full ingredient lists in each product section above.
